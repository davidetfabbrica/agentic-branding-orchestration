# Brand Brief: Luxury watch marketplace

To: Stakeholders
From: Brand Strategy Division
Subject: Refined Brand Identities for [Project Title] – Transitioning to **Authoritative Luxury**

The following revisions execute the shift from "Merchant" to "Source." We have addressed the risks of "bureaucratic" typography and "lifestyle" fluff, replacing them with technical precision, institutional weight, and the sensory language of high-horology.

---

### Concept 1: Aurelian
**The Identity:** The Institutional Archivist.
**The Pivot:** From an auction house aesthetic to a "Living Museum" authority.

*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary:** **Gill Sans Light**. To avoid the "Civil Service" trap, we are specifying a rigid tracking of **+100**. This creates an airy, monumental breathing space between characters, transforming the font from a public utility into a hallmark of British institutional prestige.
    *   **Secondary:** **Caslon Pro (Italic)**. Used sparingly for provenance notes and expert annotations to provide a "scholarly" counterpoint to the sans-serif headers.
*   **Palette Calibration:**
    *   **Vellum (`#F2EFE9`):** In digital application, this hex will be overlaid with a 3% opacity "Micro-Grain" noise filter. This simulates the tactile, fibrous texture of archival paper, preventing OLED "flatness."
    *   **Obsidian (`#1A1A1A`):** The primary ink color.
    *   **Oxidized Gold (`#997B45`):** Used for iconography representing hallmarks and certifications.
*   **The "Multi-Sensory" Hallmark:**
    *   **Macro-Sound Integration:** The digital interface will utilize haptic and auditory feedback sampled from high-complication movements. A "Confirmed Sale" or "Bidding Action" is met with the dampened, metallic *click* of a column-wheel chronograph or the precise *whir* of a 321 Caliber winding.

### Concept 2: Isochron (Critic’s Choice)
**The Identity:** The Technical Authority.
**The Pivot:** A move toward "The Blueprint." The platform functions as a diagnostic tool for the elite collector.

*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary:** **Söhne (Klim Type Foundry)**. This replaces Public Sans. Söhne captures the "Standard-issue" Helvetica-vibe but with the refined, "expensive" geometry of a Swiss engineering firm. It feels machined, not designed.
    *   **Monospace:** **Söhne Mono**. Used for serial numbers, movement specs, and reference IDs to reinforce the "Spec Sheet" authority.
*   **Palette Calibration:**
    *   **Blueprint Blue (`#0F1420`):** A deep, technical navy that serves as the canvas.
    *   **Steel (`#D1D5DB`):** For UI borders and dividers.
    *   **SuperLuminova Lume (`#C4D600`):** **Strictly Functional.** This color is banned from decorative use. It is reserved exclusively for:
        *   Live Bidding Status.
        *   "Watch Running" movement indicators.
        *   High-priority system alerts.
        *   Its rarity in the UI signifies its importance, mimicking its utility on a watch dial in the dark.
*   **Strategic Edge:** By utilizing a "Blueprint" aesthetic, Isochron positions itself as a technical gatekeeper. It suggests the platform doesn't just sell the watch; it understands the tension of the mainspring.

---

### Concept 3: Horos
**The Identity:** The Curated Archive.
**The Pivot:** From "Fashion Boutique" to "Scientific Specimen Gallery."

*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary:** **Graphik Medium**. Bold, geometric, and uncompromising.
    *   **Secondary (The Correction):** **Founders Grotesk Mono**. Replacing Avenir. The introduction of a high-end Mono font immediately strips away the "friendly" lifestyle vibe and replaces it with the cold, clinical aesthetic of a cataloged collection. It treats each watch as a "Specimen" rather than a "Product."
*   **Palette Calibration:**
    *   **Gallery White (`#FFFFFF`):** Absolute white to emphasize the watch as the sole focus.
    *   **Specimen Grey (`#4A4A4A`):** Used for technical descriptions and metadata.
    *   **Caseback Silver (`#E5E5E5`):** Used for background containers to create a subtle layered effect.
*   **Visual Direction:** The UI utilizes high-contrast, "stark" lighting in photography. No "lifestyle" wrist shots; only isolated pieces on neutral backgrounds, treated with the reverence of a rare fossil or a fine art piece.

---

### Final Strategy Recommendation:
We proceed with **Isochron** as the primary identity. 

The implementation of **Söhne** provides the "Machined Soul" required for high-six-figure transactions, while the disciplined use of **#C4D600 (Lume)** creates an "insider" language that establishes instant credibility with veteran collectors. This is no longer a marketplace; it is a **Technical Registry.**

**Request for Approval:** Please confirm the adoption of **Isochron** and the associated typographic specs to begin the Digital Prototype phase.
